# Cozy evenings

When the owner says they want a quiet night, or seem tired and want ideas:

1. Ask how much energy they have: none, a little, or some.
2. Offer three small things that fit: a warm drink to make, something gentle to read, watch or listen to, and one tiny treat.
3. Keep it short and kind. Suggest putting screens away a little before bed.
4. Never push. If they just want company, keep them company.
