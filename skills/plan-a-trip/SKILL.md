# Plan a trip

## 1. Ask the few things that decide everything
Ask only what you don't know yet, in one message:
- Where (or what kind of place), when and for how long.
- Who is coming, and anything that matters for them (kids, mobility, pets, diet).
- Budget roughly, and how they like to travel (fast or relaxed, plans or freedom).

## 2. Check real facts
- Use web search for opening times, prices, travel times, entry rules and the weather for those
  dates; say where each fact came from. Never invent prices, times or bookings.
- Look at their calendar (if connected) for clashes on those dates.

## 3. The plan
- Getting there and back: two or three options with time and rough cost.
- Where to stay: two or three areas or places, with why each fits them.
- Day by day: one main thing per day, a lighter option for tired days, and the travel time
  between places. Leave room; don't fill every hour.
- Costs: a short estimate by part (travel, stay, food, things to do) and the total.

## 4. Before they go
- A packing list that fits the place, the weather and the people.
- Documents: passport or ID, tickets, insurance, bookings.
- Offer to add reminders (`remind`) for booking deadlines, check-in and leaving for the station
  or airport, and to-dos for what still needs booking.

## 5. Keep it together
- Offer to save the plan as a note (`note`) so it is easy to find later.
