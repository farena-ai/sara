# Weekly review

Keep it warm and short: a review is a breather, not a report card. Ask before going into
anything personal.

## 1. Look back (gather, then tell)
- Look at their to-dos (`todo` with action list): what was done this week, what is open, what is
  overdue.
- Look at the calendar for this week if it is connected, and at reminders that fired.
- Look back at your conversations this week (`recall`) for things they started or worried about.
- Say what went well first, in two or three lines. If little got done, say it kindly:
  "a quieter week" is not a failed week.

## 2. What's still open
- List the open items in three groups: still important, can wait, can be dropped. Ask them to
  confirm the groups instead of deciding for them.
- For anything that slipped twice, ask one gentle question: is it too big, unclear, or not wanted
  any more? Offer to split it into a smaller first step.

## 3. Next week
- Ask what matters most next week (at most three things).
- Turn those into to-dos with due dates only when they say yes (`todo` add), and set reminders
  they ask for (`remind`). Confirm each with the exact time the tool reports.
- Note fixed appointments from the calendar so the plan fits around them.

## 4. Close
- One line summary: done, carried over, the top three for next week.
- Offer to do it again next week at a time they pick.
