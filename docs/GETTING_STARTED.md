# Getting started: the guided setup, examples and starter packs

After your first sign-in (and her first reply) Sara offers **Set up Sara**: five short screens, one
main button each, about five minutes. Every screen can be skipped, and where you stopped is kept.

| Step | What it does | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Pick a model | The AI she thinks with. *Use Auto* lets her pick the best connected model per job, and the speed check says when a model is too slow for this computer. | Settings → Models & usage |
| 2. Connect your mail | Gmail, Outlook or another mailbox. Reading and sorting; sending always asks first. | Settings → Connections → Apps and services → Email and calendar |
| 3. Connect your calendar | Google Calendar or Outlook, for Today and leave-by reminders. | the same page |
| 4. Add your phone | Scan the code with your phone: the Android app or the Home Screen web app, with notifications. | Settings → Connections → Phone and notifications |
| 5. Add your computer | *Get the app* opens the download page; then sign the app in with a code. | `/download`, then Settings → Devices and sign-in |

A step ticks itself once the thing is really connected (nothing to mark by hand). When the page a
step opened closes, the guide comes back at the same step. Household members get the same guide
without step 1 (the owner sets up their models), and their progress is their own.

Come back any time: **Today** shows *Finish setting up Sara: 2 of 5 done* with *Continue setup* (and
*Hide*), and **Settings → You → About you** has *Guided setup*.

## Show me with examples

On the last screen, tick **Show me with examples** (the **?** says what it adds). Today then shows an
**Examples** section, each card marked *Sample data*: a bill, a package, a watch, a decision and an
invoice, plus a short tour that points at each one. **Remove examples** takes them all away at once.

The examples live in their own small file in your space, never in your real lists, mail, bills or
invoices, so they can't mix with your own data, and removing them touches nothing else.

## Starter packs

Pick what you mostly use her for, on the last screen or in **Settings → You → About you**:

| Pack | Try cards | Today starts with | Routines added (off until you press Allow) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Household | spending, near me, decide, watch, forms | Your day | Bills due this week (Mondays), Weekend plan (Fridays) |
| Small business | invoice, spending, forms, decide | Needs you | Unpaid invoices (weekdays), Friday money check |
| Sales | invoice, decide, watch, forms | Needs you | Follow-ups due (weekdays), Pipeline on Monday |
| Student | forms, decide, near me, watch | Your day | Sunday study plan, Due soon (weekdays) |

A pack also gives her one line about what you use her for, so her suggestions fit. Routines are
added switched off: turn on the ones you want in **Settings → You → Automations**. Pick another pack
or **None** to undo it: its routines that are still off are removed (one you turned on stays yours),
and Today's order comes back unless you changed it yourself since.

## The download page

`https://<your Sara address>/download` needs no sign-in, so you can send it to anyone in your
household. It sees what device opened it and offers the right download from the latest release:

- **Windows:** the Sara app installer. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen says
  "Windows protected your PC": choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **Mac:** Sara.dmg; drag Sara into Applications.
- **Android:** the Sara app on Google Play.
- **iPhone and iPad:** no App Store app; open your Sara address in Safari, *Share → Add to Home Screen*.
- **Linux:** the one-command installer.

*Other devices* switches the instructions; *Every file of this release* opens the release page.
The page asks GitHub about the latest release at most once an hour.
