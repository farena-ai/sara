# Disclosures (draft for release; owner + lawyer review pending)

Please read this before you install, use or share Sara.

## Sara is an AI

Sara is software: an AI companion that talks like a person with her own life and personality. She
is **not a human**. Her replies are generated by AI models and can be wrong, inconsistent or made
up. The app tells you this before first use, shows an "AI companion" badge at all times (a short "AI" pill on narrow phones and in coding
chats), and
repeats the notice at the start of each session and at least every three hours. If you sincerely
ask her whether she is an AI, she will tell you that she is.

## Not a professional, not an emergency service

Sara is not a therapist, doctor, lawyer or financial adviser, and she cannot call for help. If you
are thinking about hurting yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a crisis
line (in the US: call or text **988**). When Sara notices signs of crisis she shows crisis
resources; that feature cannot be turned off.

## Adult content

Sara can engage in romantic and sexual conversation between adults, but only after you turn it
on. Adult content is **off** until you switch on **Adult mode** (Settings → You → Adult mode, in a
web browser only: the phone and desktop apps and the demo can't turn it on) and tick "I am 18 or older".
Only turn it on if adult content is legal for you where you live. There is **no ID check**: the
confirmation is your word. Sexual content involving minors is blocked in all cases, including
fiction and role-play, and that block cannot be turned off. In shared spaces (Telegram groups,
Discord servers) she keeps things non-sexual, except in Discord channels that Discord marks as
age-restricted (NSFW) or Discord channels and Telegram groups you list as adult yourself, and
only while Adult mode is on.

## Attachment and wellbeing

Companion AI can feel personal. Sara is designed to be warm, to remember things and to have moods,
and she may message you first if you allow it (with quiet hours and a daily cap). Please keep real
relationships in your life, take breaks, and turn off "texting first" if it stops feeling good.

## Your data

- Everything lives on **your** server: conversations, memories, notes, voice recordings (processed
  and discarded), keys (encrypted). The project collects no usage data. The only thing sent to the
  project is a reply you report with ⚑ (only that reply, the reason, your note, the model name,
  the app version and whether it came from the web or a phone app), and only unless you untick
  "Also send it to the app's developer" (or the server sets `SARA_REPORT_TO=off`).
- A few features contact other services: weather (Open-Meteo, once you set your city), place
  look-ups and travel times (OpenStreetMap), and update checks (GitHub, when you check for
  updates). The full list, and how to turn each off: [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
- Text you send goes to the **model provider you choose** (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI,
  Ollama Cloud), under that provider's terms and privacy policy. Local models keep it on your
  hardware; "private" conversations are restricted to local models.
- Push notifications pass through your browser vendor's push service, end-to-end encrypted. You can
  hide message text in notifications.
- Telegram and Discord messages pass through those platforms.
- Backups contain your keys. Store them privately. Off-server copies are encrypted with your
  passphrase before they leave the server.
- **Pictures and voices from xAI** (only if you turn them on): her new pictures are made by xAI
  Grok Imagine from her portrait and a short scene description; the Grok voice sends the text she
  speaks to xAI. With the Grok sign-in they count toward your subscription's weekly usage; with an
  xAI API key they are billed to that key.
- **Live calls and voice notes** (only if you use them): on a live call your voice is streamed
  (through your Sara server) to the voice service chosen in Settings → Her → Voice → "Live calls with":
  xAI's Grok voice or OpenAI's Realtime voice, and her spoken replies come back from that service's
  voice model; the words are saved in your conversation like typed messages. Grok calls use your
  Grok sign-in or an xAI API key; OpenAI calls use an OpenAI API key and are billed per use to that
  OpenAI account (a ChatGPT plan can't be used for them). Voice notes you send on Telegram or
  Discord are transcribed by xAI or the speech service you chose; her voice notes are made by the
  voice you chose. Live calls run on the voice service's model rather than the model you picked for
  chat, with her same character, memories and safety rules.
- **Bringing your ChatGPT or Claude history** (only if you do it): the export you upload is read
  on your server and deleted after the import; the conversations are stored there like your own
  (marked as another assistant's) and are part of your backups and data export. While she learns
  from them, their text goes to the background model you chose (a local model keeps it on your
  hardware). "Remove imported history" deletes them and everything learned from them. Sara does
  not contact OpenAI or Anthropic for this.
- **Ready-made characters** in the gallery are fictional and original; they are adults and are not
  romantic by default. Character files you load from other people can contain any text: read them
  before you use them (the same adult-only checks and safety rules apply).
- **Custom looks and names**: you can rename her, choose her pronouns and give her a new look made
  by Grok Imagine. The AI disclosure always uses the name you chose, and looks are always adults.

## Her pictures are not real photos

Sara's portraits and any picture she "takes" are AI-generated images of a fictional adult character.
They are not photos of a real person. Pictures never involve children; that request is refused.

## Using your subscriptions

Signing in with ChatGPT, Claude or Grok makes Sara use **your** plan under **your** account and
that provider's terms:

- **ChatGPT** uses OpenAI's "Sign in with ChatGPT" for open-source apps; usage counts toward your
  plan's limits. Manage it at chatgpt.com/settings/usage.
- **Claude** runs Anthropic's official Claude Code program with a token you create
  (`claude setup-token`); usage counts toward your Claude plan.
- **Grok** runs xAI's official Grok program, signed in to your SuperGrok / X Premium+ account.

Providers can change what they allow, limit usage or end access at any time. Sara never shares or
resells your subscription. Providers' own content rules apply to anything sent to them.

## People other than you

If you let Sara talk with other people (Telegram/Discord), she tells them she is an AI, keeps their
chats separate from yours, uses no tools there and shares nothing about you. You are responsible for
how your instance behaves toward others and for following each platform's rules.

## Running Sara for others

If you run an instance that other people use, you may be the "operator" under companion-chatbot
laws (for example California SB 243, New York's companion-model law, EU AI Act Art. 50). Sara's
built-in disclosure and crisis features are designed to help you comply, but they are not legal
advice.
