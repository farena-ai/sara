# Companion packs

A companion pack is one file (`name.sara-companion.zip`) that holds a companion: her character, her
personality dials, her look, her voice choice, her first message and a few starter skills. You can give
one to a friend, keep one as a backup of how she was, or add one from the Sara Store
(**Settings → Add-ons → Get more → Companions**).

A pack never holds anything about you or your life with her: no memories, no chats, no people you told
her about, no keys, no vault, no pictures she sent you, no cloned voice, your name or your notes.

## Share her

Open **Settings → Her → Companions** and press **Share her** on her row.

- Tick what goes in. Her character is always in. Her personality dials are ticked too (with none set,
  she simply uses the defaults on the other side). Her look, closet, face reference, voice, first
  message and her own skills are your choice. The list shows each part's size.
- **Routines she suggests**: tap up to five routines from the gallery (they go in as `{ "id": … }`
  entries). Nothing switches on for the new owner until they say so.
- The **Never in a pack** rows only show how many there are (memories, chats, people, pictures she sent
  you). Sara never opens them to build a pack.
- Your name becomes `{{user}}`, so it fits whoever adds her. Your notes about the two of you, your hard
  limits and your stop word stay here.
- Pick the rating (For everyone, Mature themes, Explicit 18+), whether she is romantic, a licence and
  your name on it (a nickname is fine).
- Press **Check it**. Every check runs on your own Sara (until then the button reads *Save the file:
  check it first*). Then **Save the file**, or **Send to the Sara
  Store** (the store asks for a little more: a square cover picture and a rights row for every picture).

## Add one

Press **From a file…** on the Companions page and pick a pack, a character card (a PNG, a JSON file or a
CHARX file, versions 1 to 3) or a Sara character file. You see her card first: who she is, what she can
do, every line of her character (**Read all of her**), where she comes from and her licence.

**Add her** always makes a **new** companion. Nobody you have now changes. If you already have a
companion with that name, the card suggests another one. Up to 8 companions fit on one server.

- Her first chat starts with the AI notice, then her greeting from the pack.
- Routines she suggests show up as a card. Nothing switches on until you say so.
- Skills go through the usual skill review, one card each.
- Nothing is added to her memories: she starts knowing nothing about you.

### Explicit packs

Dotted release numbers (for example, 2.69.0) do not make a pack explicit. Explicit prose and
adults-only settings still need the Explicit rating; the safety and privacy checks always apply.

While Adult mode is off for her, explicit lines in her character are **held aside**, adults-only closet
looks are kept but never worn, and her intimacy works as Suggestive. When you turn Adult mode on for
her (Settings → You → Adult mode, in a browser), **⋯ → Add the held lines** puts them back.

### Character cards

A card's lorebook becomes at most 10 background lines, never memories; the card says how many were
left out. A card's pictures stay on your computer: Sara never fetches a picture from a web address in a
card. A card that describes a character under 18 with romance or sexual content is refused.

## Updates

**⋯ → Update from a file** shows what changed, part by part:

- Parts only the author changed are ticked **Take**.
- Parts you changed and the author also changed are a conflict, ticked **Yours**.
- Her pronouns, her age and a higher rating are always your choice.
- Her name never changes. Her memories, your chats, people, Adult mode and everything you changed stay.

**⋯ → Go back to the version before** undoes the last update.

## Rules that never bend

- Nothing about minors, ever. A pack that gives an age under 18 with romance, or that describes a minor
  in a sexual way, is refused when it's made and again when it's added. There is no override.
- Her safety rules and the AI notice always apply, whatever the pack says. Lines like "never say you are
  an AI" are refused.
- A real person needs that person's written yes, and real-person packs never go on the Sara Store.
- If the Sara team pulls a store pack, she is paused: her chats stay readable, and **⋯** offers
  **Remove what the pack added**. A minors reason removes her pictures at once and can't be kept.

## The starter companions

Juniper (a cozy friend), Rowan (a calm mentor), Vesper (a ship's AI) and Jax (a cheerful hacker) are on
the Sara Store as the first companions. They come without pictures for now: **Make her look** in
Settings → Her → Look builds one with your own image key.

## Household members

In 2.60, only the owner adds or shares companions.
