page fills its rows from JS (lists, cards, setup forms), so the markup count is low on purpose.

## Overlaps (several pages about one thing)

- **Bringing things in:** `her-import` Bring her from another app, `dh-data` Export and import, `dh-movein` Move in from another agent. Three pages import history (companion apps, ChatGPT/Claude, other agents); Companions also has a "Bring your companion" section. One page, three sections.
- **Budgets and spend:** `ab-budgets` Budgets, `dh-usage` Usage and costs, `dh-usage-report` Usage reports. Per-reply ceilings, spend limits and monthly budgets live on three pages. Limits move into Usage and costs; Usage reports stays (members see it).
- **Health and readiness:** `dh-health` Health, `dh-quality` How she did, `dh-recovery` Readiness and recovery, `dh-operations` Operations, `dh-safety` Safety check-up. Four read-mostly status pages. Health hosts the three thin ones as sections; Safety check-up stays and feeds Home.
- **Email:** `ab-mail` Email and calendar, `ab-google` Gmail and Calendar, `co-email` Email. Your mailboxes, the Google account, and her own mailbox. One "Email and calendar" tile (Google as its detail); her mailbox stays a chat-app tile, renamed "Her own email" in the tile caption only.
- **Briefings and reminders:** `you-briefing` Morning briefing, `you-leave-by` Leave-by reminders, `ab-when` When it comes up, `her-calls` Calls from her. Three small reminder pages in You. One page with three sections; Calls from her links to it.
- **This computer:** `desktop-app` This computer, `co-computer` Computer use, `models-local` On this computer. "This computer", "Computer use" (whose first heading is also "This computer") and "On this computer". Kept apart, adjacent, with distinct one-line leads.
- **Phone:** `co-phone` Phone and notifications, `co-shortcuts` iPhone Shortcuts, `dh-devices` Devices and sign-in, `her-calls` Calls from her. iPhone Shortcuts (5 settings) becomes a section of Phone and notifications.
- **Pick up and continuity:** `co-pickup` Pick up across apps, `co-continuity` Session continuity. Both about sharing context between apps. One page.
- **Model sources:** `models-providers` Model providers, `models-subs` Subscriptions. Subscriptions are a kind of provider. One page "Providers and plans".
- **Plugins and tool servers:** `ab-plugins` Plugins, `ab-mcp` Other programs (MCP), `ab-store` Get more. Kept apart (different jobs) but adjacent; the store links both.
- **Reports:** `dh-reports` Reports, `dh-trust` Trust report, `dh-quality` How she did. Three things called a report. Kept (different audiences); leads say which is which.
