# How do I…?

<!-- Generated from web/howto.json by node scripts/gen-guide.ts. Edit howto.json, not this file. -->

Every page of the app has a **How do I…?** box at the top with short, step-by-step answers. These are all 135 of
them in one place. In the app, open the box and press **Show me** to see the control it talks about. New here? Start with
[Start here](START_HERE.md); writing code with her? [For coders](FOR_CODERS.md).

## Chat and the main views

### Chat

**How do I start?**

1. Type in the box at the bottom and press Enter.
2. Or tap one of the suggestions above.

**How do I send her a photo or a file?**

1. Press the paperclip next to the box.
2. Pick the file; add a line if you like.

**How do I switch this chat to coding?**

1. Press the chip at the top of the chat.
2. Pick a workspace and switch the mode to coding.

### Today

**How do I plan my day with her?**

1. Press Plan my day with her.
2. She looks at your list, calendar and weather.

**How do I change what Today shows?**

1. Press Customise.
2. Switch sections on or off and drag them in order.

### Planner

**How do I add a to-do?**

1. Type it in the box under To-do and press Add.
2. Or just tell her in chat: "add milk to my list".

**How do I set a reminder?**

1. Open Reminders, type what and when.
2. Or tell her: "remind me at 5 to call Ana".

**How do I review my week?**

1. Press Review my week.

### Memory

**How do I tell her something to remember?**

1. Say it in chat: "remember that I'm allergic to nuts".
2. Or add a note here.

**How do I make her forget something?**

1. Find the note (search helps).
2. Press Forget on it.

### Library

**How do I give her a document?**

1. Drop the file on the box at the top, or click it to pick one.
2. She reads it and keeps the key points.

**How do I ask about a document?**

1. Ask in any chat: "what does the lease say about pets?"
2. She quotes the part she used.

### Scrapbook

**How do I pin a message?**

1. Press 📌 under a message.
2. It shows here with the pins and inside jokes.

**How do I keep an inside joke?**

1. Tell her: "that's our inside joke now".
2. Or add it here.

### Our moments

**How do I keep a moment?**

1. Press ♡ under a message or a photo.
2. It shows here with a caption she writes.

**How do I choose which photos land here?**

1. Pick under Add photos: all, only kept moments, or off.

### Your story

**How do I get a look back?**

1. Pick the dates under Look back.
2. Press the button; she writes it from what's on this page.

**How do I see where something came from?**

1. Click any entry.
2. The chat or note it came from opens.

### Team

**How do I give the team a job?**

1. Type the goal in the box.
2. Press Start; she plans and hands parts to helpers.

**How do I stop a helper?**

1. Open the task.
2. Press Stop next to the helper, or stop the whole task.

### Findings

**Where do findings come from?**

1. Her security pass after coding turns adds them.
2. Dependency checks and sandbox tries add them too.

**How do I fix one?**

1. Open the finding.
2. Press Fix on a branch: she tries it on its own branch.

### Evals

**How do I try other settings?**

1. Under Try other settings, pick past tasks and variants.
2. Check the estimate, then start.

**How do I read the result?**

1. Each variant is scored next to your settings.
2. A winner is named only when the evidence says so.

## Settings → Her

### Name and personality

**How do I change her name?**

1. Type the new name in Name at the top of this page.
2. Click anywhere outside the box: it saves by itself.
3. Her notes about herself follow the new name.

**How do I make her my own?**

1. Press ✨ Create your companion for a guided setup (about 5 minutes).
2. Or pick a character under Start from a character and press Use this.
3. Nudge the personality dials until the sample line sounds like her.

**How do I go back to how she was?**

1. Press Undo changes in the bar at the bottom.
2. It brings back how she was when you opened this page.

### Relationship

**How do I change what we are to each other?**

1. Pick a card under Your relationship.
2. Add your own words in the box below it if you like.
3. Press Try it to hear how she'd react.

**How do I make her less jealous?**

1. Find Jealousy under the dials.
2. Move it to the left; the line above it shows how she'd sound.
3. She is never controlling, whatever the dial says.

### Look

**How do I give her a new face?**

1. Describe the look and pick a Style.
2. Press Make a picture, then Use this look when you like it.
3. Or press Bring my picture to life to start from a photo.

**How do I change her outfit?**

1. Under Outfits and places, describe the outfit.
2. Press Try it on.
3. Keep favourites in the Closet.

### Voice

**How do I pick her voice?**

1. Choose a voice under Her voice.
2. Press Preview to hear it.
3. It saves by itself; voice notes and calls use it.

**How do I talk to her out loud?**

1. Choose how she hears you under Hearing you.
2. Then press the microphone in the chat box and speak.

### Calls from her

**How do I choose when she may call me?**

1. Tick what may ring you: reminders, finished tasks, the morning briefing.
2. Set quiet hours and a limit for calls a day.
3. Press Call me now to try it.

**How do I stop her calling?**

1. Untick every reason at the top of this page.
2. She can still send messages; only calls stop.

### Pictures from her

**How do I let her send pictures?**

1. Pick a way under Pictures.
2. For Grok Imagine, paste an xAI API key.
3. Ask her in chat: "send me a selfie".

**How do I stop pictures?**

1. Set Pictures to Off.
2. She will describe instead of sending a picture.

### Companions

**How do I add another companion?**

1. Type a name under Add a companion and pick where to start from.
2. Press Add.
3. Switch between companions from the sidebar.

**How do companions share the Planner?**

1. Under Planner, pick whether to-dos, reminders and notes are shared or their own.
2. Memories and chats always stay with each companion.

### Bring her from another app

**How do I bring her from another app?**

1. Export your data from that app (ChatGPT, Claude, a character app).
2. Choose the file under Choose the file.
3. Tick what to keep, then press Let her read them.

**How do I throw an import away?**

1. Before saving, press Never mind.
2. After a read, press Throw it all away.

### Coding projects

**How do I point her at my project?**

1. Pick a Workspace (your projects folder).

**How do I make her try commands safely?**

1. Switch on Try commands in a sandbox first.

**How do I check before I ship?**

1. Press Before you ship.

### Budgets

**How do I limit what a coding reply may use?**

1. Under Coding chats, set Tokens, Steps or a Spending limit.
2. She stops and asks when a reply reaches it.

**What happens near the limit?**

1. Choose under Near the limit whether she asks or stops.
2. Usage and costs shows what was used.

### Keep going and repeats

**How do I make her keep going until the tests pass?**

1. In the chat, press + and pick Keep going until it’s done….
2. Under Done when, pick the check (the project’s tests are offered first).
3. Press Start. She works in rounds; Sara runs the check after each one.

**How do I have her check something every few minutes?**

1. In the chat, press + and pick Repeat this… (or type /loop every 10m: what to check).
2. Pick how often and until when, then press Start.
3. She only tells you when something changes; Planner → Repeats lists them all.

### When it comes up

**How do I have her remind me when someone comes up?**

1. In any of your chats, say “when Dan comes up, remind me he owes me $20” (or type /when Dan: he owes me $20).
2. A card under her reply shows it, with Undo.
3. The next time you mention Dan in one of your own chats, she brings it up once.

**How do I see or forget what she is waiting to bring up?**

1. Open Planner → Reminders → When it comes up.
2. Press Forget next to one; Done lists the ones that already came up.
3. Or type /when for the list and /when forget 1.

### GitHub

**How do I add a GitHub token?**

1. Make a token on GitHub (Settings → Developer settings).
2. Paste it in GitHub token.
3. Press Save.

**How do I turn issues into tasks?**

1. Under Issues become tasks, set a label.
2. Press Look now to fetch them.

### Servers over SSH

**How do I connect a server?**

1. Type the server address and your user name under Connect a server.
2. Press Connect and wait while Sara looks at it.
3. Say yes to its ID card, then follow the way in she picks.

**How do I connect a cPanel server?**

1. In cPanel, make an API token called sara that expires tomorrow.
2. Copy it and paste it into the cPanel token box.
3. Press Install my key: Sara installs her key and deletes the token.

**What if it doesn't work?**

1. Read the box under the button you pressed.
2. Press the button it offers, like Find the port or Check now.

**Does she ask before running commands there?**

1. Yes, every command asks first by default.
2. Change it in Tools and approvals.

**Why didn't she answer my email?**

2. Press Check my sender in Email settings and send her a mail.
