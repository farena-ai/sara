Privacy policy

Sara, the self-hosted AI companion and assistant, and its iPhone and Android apps. Last updated: November 9, 2026.

The short version

  • Sara runs on your own server (your computer, a home server or a server you rent). Your conversations, memories, plans, photos and settings are stored there, not with us.
  • The phone apps only connect to the server address you enter (or to our demo server if you choose Try the demo).
  • We don't sell data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics in the apps.
  • The only thing that reaches us is a reply you choose to report (see below).

What your own server stores

Everything you use Sara for: conversations, what she remembers about you, to-dos and reminders, photos you send or she makes, voice settings and connections you set up. It stays on your server and in the backups you choose to make. You can export it or delete it at any time (Settings → Data & health). Conversations marked off the record are never written to disk.

Services you choose to connect

Your server sends data to outside services only when you set them up, and only what each needs:

  • AI model providers (for example a model on your own computer, or a cloud provider or subscription you sign in to): the conversation she is answering, so the model can write her reply. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Google (Gmail, Calendar) and GitHub, if you connect them: only what she reads or does for you, with your approval for anything that sends or changes something.
  • Notifications: Apple (iPhone) or your browser's push service delivers notifications from your server. Web notifications are end-to-end encrypted. You can choose to leave the message text out of notifications.
  • Off-site backups, if you set them up: encrypted on your server before they leave it.

The phone apps

  • The apps show your server's Sara in a window and add notifications, widgets, sharing to her, calls and an app lock. They store the server's address and your sign-in on the phone.
  • Microphone and camera are used only when you start a voice note, a call or take a photo for her. Call audio goes to your server, which relays it to the voice service you set up.
  • Location (Android place reminders, optional). If you turn on place reminders, the Android app accesses your phone's location, including in the background when the app is closed or not in use, so it can remind you when you arrive at or leave a place you saved (for example "remind me to water the plants when I get home"). The app shows an explanation before Android asks for permission, and the feature is off until you turn it on. Your location is checked on the phone against the places you saved; the app sends only the event ("arrived at Home", "left Work") to your own Sara server, which keeps your saved places. Your location is not sent to us, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising or analytics. If you answer a request from Sara for your location (the phone helper), the app sends your approximate or precise location once, only to your own server, only when you allow it. Turn place reminders off or remove location permission at any time in Settings → Connections → Phone and notifications, or in Android's settings.
  • Face ID / fingerprint (if you turn on the app lock) is checked by your phone; the app never sees it.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking libraries are included.

The demo server

If you choose Try the demo, you chat with our demo server. Demo conversations are kept in memory only, are never saved to disk, are visible only to you, and disappear after a few hours of inactivity. Please don't share personal information there. Replies come from an AI model run by our server's model provider.

Reporting a reply

You can report any reply with ⚑. The report is kept on your server. Unless you untick it, it is also sent to us as the app's developer, containing only that reply's text, the reason you chose and your note (plus the app platform, version and model name), so we can improve safety. We keep reports for up to two years and don't link them to you.

Children

Sara is meant for adults. It is not directed at children, and sexual content involving minors is always blocked.

Your choices

Since your data is on your server, you control it: export, delete, or uninstall at any time. To ask us about a report you sent, or anything in this policy, email the developer at sara@backstagecoding.com (see also the support page).
